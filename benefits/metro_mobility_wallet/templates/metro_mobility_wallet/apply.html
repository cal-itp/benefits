Don’t have a Login.gov account?

If you don't have a Login.gov account, we'll have you create one to get started.

You’ll need to complete two steps.

  1. Create a Login.gov account
    1. Click “Apply with Login.gov”
    2. Enter a personal email address.
    3. Enter a password.
    4. Select a way to authenticate your account
  2. Verify your identity

    After you create your Login.gov account, you have to verify your identity. The process asks you to:

    1. Upload a picture of your ID
    2. Enter your Social Security number.
    3. Verify your phone number.
