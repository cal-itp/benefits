California Integrated Travel Project

    Cal-ITP Benefits addresses several of Cal-ITP's key goals to improve transit throughout California:

    • Promote equitable access to transit
    • Lower costs
    • Increase Ridership
    • Improve customer experience

    The project also, however, seeks to expand adoption of open-loop payments and supports efforts to provide accurate and complete information for planning trips on public transit in real-time. Learn more about Cal-ITP.
